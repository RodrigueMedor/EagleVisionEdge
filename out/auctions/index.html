Auction FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our vehicle auctions

How do I place a bid?

Click on any active auction, enter your bid amount, and confirm. You must be logged in and verified to bid.

What is a reserve price?

A reserve price is the minimum price the seller will accept. The auction will not end below this price.

Can I retract a bid?

Once placed, bids cannot be retracted. Please bid carefully and only if you intend to purchase the vehicle.

What happens if I win?

You'll receive an email with payment instructions and vehicle pickup details. Payment is typically due within 3 days.
