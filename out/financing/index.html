Frequently Asked Questions

Financing Questions?

Everything you need to know about our financing options

What credit score do I need for financing?

We work with all credit scores. Excellent credit (750+) gets the best rates, but we have options for fair and building credit profiles.

How much down payment is required?

Down payments range from 10% to 25% depending on your credit profile and the vehicle price.

Can I pay off my loan early?

Yes! All our loans have no prepayment penalties, so you can pay off your loan early without any extra fees.

What documents do I need?

You'll need a valid driver's license, proof of income (pay stubs), proof of residence, and insurance.

How long does approval take?

Pre-qualification takes just a few minutes. Full approval typically takes 24-48 hours once documents are submitted.
