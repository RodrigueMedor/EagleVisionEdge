Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

Everything you need to know about our rental services

What are the requirements to rent a vehicle?

You must be 21+ years old, have a valid driver's license, clean driving record, and pass a background check. Commercial rentals may require additional documentation.

Is insurance included in the rental price?

Basic insurance is included with Economy and Premium plans. Commercial insurance is included with Cargo Van rentals. Additional coverage options are available.

Can I rent for less than a week?

Our standard rental periods are weekly, but we offer flexible options for longer commitments. Contact us for custom rental periods.

What happens if the vehicle needs maintenance?

All vehicles include regular maintenance. If issues arise, we provide 24/7 roadside assistance and replacement vehicles when needed.

Are there mileage restrictions?

All our rental plans include unlimited miles, perfect for rideshare and delivery drivers.
